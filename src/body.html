        Four rules the product doesn’t break

        Intent before logistics

        Start with what you’d be down for, not a calendar invite. Times stay loose until there’s actually a plan.

        Nobody has to go first

        “Still down?” is private. Nobody ever sees a no, so saying yes costs nothing.

        Built to get you out

        No feed to scroll, no likes to chase. Pulse counts plans that happened, not time in the app.

        Private by design

        Friends you add and clubs you join, never strangers. Rooms clear two weeks after. No ads, never sold.

        Why this gets big

        WEDGE

        It works with one friend group

        Two yeses make a plan. Pulse is useful to six friends on day one, so it doesn’t need a million users to feel alive.

        EXPANSION

        Clubs turn acquaintances into friends

        Pulse Pods give a run club, class or team its own signals. Pair Pulse schedules coffee chats around times both people are already free.
