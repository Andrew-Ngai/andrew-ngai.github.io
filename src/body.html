      Situational Awareness, graded two years in

      In June 2024 Leopold Aschenbrenner published Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead. I agree with more of it than most people I talk to. Here are my notes comparing five of its calls with September 2026, and where I’d grade it differently than its fans or its critics.

      The call (2024)What happenedMy grade

      Power becomes the binding constraint.

      GE Vernova’s gas turbine backlog reached 116 GW in Q2 2026, and it’s already taking reservations for 2031 deliveries.Utility Dive, July 2026

      Confirmed

      About $1T a year of AI investment by 2027.

      Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft and Meta planned more than $700B of 2026 capex. Alphabet then raised its own guide to as much as $205B in July.Sherwood News, W.Media

      On pace

      Automate AI research first.

      OpenAI says it hit its goal of an automated research intern in September 2026, and is aiming for an automated AI researcher by March 2028.OpenAI, September 2026

      AdoptedMy take

      A ~1 GW training cluster around 2026.

      Stargate Abilene was running about 0.3 GW in April 2026, building toward 1.2 GW.Epoch AI, April 2026

      Arriving in stages

      One giant cluster at the top of the ladder.

      Stargate is seven US sites aiming for 9+ GW by 2029, and Abilene’s planned expansion went to other sites instead.Epoch AI, April 2026
